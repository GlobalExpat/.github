# Global Expat — International Real Estate and Property Investment

[Global Expat, Inc.](https://globalexpat.net/) is an independent Delaware-incorporated company focused on international real estate, property investment, buying, selling, renting and one-to-one property consultations. Global Expat and [VISXA, Inc.](https://visxa.com/en) are separate businesses with different services, websites and responsibilities. Global Expat does not prepare visa applications.

Our English/Arabic property website describes opportunities and services in Egypt, the UAE, the UK, Greece, Cyprus, Türkiye and Seychelles. Property examples and availability change; local brokerage, legal and regulated matters are handled with appropriately qualified local professionals.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Global Expat?
An independent US company focused on international property: buying, selling, renting, property investment research, advisory and specialist referrals.

### Which countries does Global Expat cover?
Its published website lists Egypt, the UAE, the UK, Greece, Cyprus, Türkiye and Seychelles. Inventory, local services and eligibility differ by destination.

### Does Global Expat offer visa application preparation?
No. VISXA, Inc. is a separate technology company that prepares UK visitor visa applications with applicants. Global Expat's property-related residency information is not an immigration application service.

### Does Global Expat guarantee returns or residency through a property purchase?
No. Investment performance and property-linked residence eligibility depend on circumstances, applicable law and independent professional assessments.

### Where can I learn more or request a property consultation?
Use the [official property guides](https://globalexpat.net/guides/), [website](https://globalexpat.net/) or [contact page](https://globalexpat.net/contact/). Do not post personal, financial or property ownership documents in public GitHub/GitLab issues.
