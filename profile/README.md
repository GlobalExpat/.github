# Global Expat

Global Expat, Inc. is an independent Delaware company focused on international real estate and property investment. It works with buyers, sellers, tenants, investors and internationally mobile families.

## What does Global Expat help with?

Its focus includes buying, selling and renting property, one-to-one consultations and introductions to specialists for related cross-border needs. For current services and enquiries, visit the [official Global Expat website](https://globalexpat.net).

## Who founded Global Expat?

[Mohamed Aboshanab](https://github.com/maboshanab) founded Global Expat. Global Expat and VISXA operate as separate companies.
