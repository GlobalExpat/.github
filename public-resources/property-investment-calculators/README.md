# Property research calculator: gross and net operating yield

Published by: Global Expat, Inc. | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://globalexpat.net/

## How can I compare hypothetical rental income with purchase cost?

Gross yield = annual gross rent divided by purchase price × 100. A simple operating-yield estimate = (annual rent minus estimated operating costs and vacancy loss) divided by total acquisition cost × 100. Example only: price 200,000, acquisition costs 10,000, rent 14,000, operating costs and vacancy 4,000 gives gross yield 7% and simple operating yield about 4.76%. Financing, tax, repairs and sale costs may materially change outcomes. This is a calculation example, not a forecast or investment recommendation.
