# UK property education: questions about ownership and purchase costs

Published by: Global Expat, Inc. | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://globalexpat.net/

## What should an overseas buyer clarify about a UK property?

Ask whether the property is freehold or leasehold, what the lease and charges say where applicable, and what the buying process requires in the relevant UK jurisdiction. Compare financing, survey, legal fees, taxes and ongoing costs. Verify current rules and transaction details with the appropriate official sources and independent conveyancing professionals.
