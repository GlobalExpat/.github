## How can I compare property abroad responsibly?

Global Expat is an independent US international real estate company. Define your intended use, budget, currency and ownership costs. Compare similar properties and distinguish asking prices from completed sales. Before paying a deposit, ask an independent local professional to verify title, seller authority, contract terms and applicable restrictions. Keep personal and financial documents private. Hypothetical rental yields are not forecasts.
