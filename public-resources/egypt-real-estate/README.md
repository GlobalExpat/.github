# Egypt real estate: a practical viewing worksheet

Published by: Global Expat, Inc. | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://globalexpat.net/

## What should renters or buyers inspect in Egypt?

Before a viewing, confirm exact location, building access, elevator, utilities, internet availability, furnishings, maintenance responsibilities and total cost. During the visit, check water pressure, noise, daylight and transport access. Ask for written terms, payment schedule and landlord or seller authority; consult an independent local professional where needed. No inventory or property availability is asserted.
