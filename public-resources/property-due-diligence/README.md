# Property due diligence: questions before you pay a deposit

Published by: Global Expat, Inc. | Date: 2026-11-26 | Official site: https://globalexpat.net/

## What questions should a buyer ask before committing funds?

Confirm seller authority, property identification, title and encumbrances, permissions, service charges, handover terms, deposit protection and refund conditions. Request independent advice on contract terms and local restrictions. Create an evidence log with question, source, date and unresolved issue. Do not share identity or ownership documents publicly.
